A LETTER FOR MY BABY

Happy birthday,
my baby. ❤️

Sometimes I still think about how crazy it is that we found each other. We started with Bumble, and honestly, our beginning was kind of a mess. You deactivated, then we talked again, then I deactivated, and, after all of that, we still ended up here. Together. And now I can't imagine my life without you in it.

I remember when we were still talking and I first started liking you. I liked that you weren't bastos. You were funny, friendly, and you talked to me without trying too hard to flirt with me. You just felt easy to talk to. I didn't know then how much you would eventually mean to me.

And then came our first date.

I still remember seeing you approaching me while holding that sunflower, looking a little nervous. You were so cute, and your smile really shined. I remember you being scared that I was going to stand you up because I kept saying I was going to be late, and honestly, I find that so cute now. We were both nervous, and it felt like I was going on my first date all over again.

You went with me to look for makeup shades, carried my things for me, and you were already so boyfriend-coded without even trying. Looking back, I think there was something about that day that made me feel warm. Like maybe this could become something.

And, it did.

There are so many things I love about you. I love how jolly you are, how makulit you are, how weird you can be, and how you make me laugh even when I don't feel like laughing. I love when you stick your tongue out. I love when you pretend to be angry. I love your smile, your pretty eyes, and especially the way your eyes sparkle when you look at me. I don't think you realize how much that gets me every single time.

But more than how cute or handsome you are, I love the way you love me.

You always make an effort. You care about me in so many little ways. You feed me, carry my things, take pictures of me, talk to me gently, kiss me on my lips and cheeks and forehead, and stay with me when I need you. Even when you don't necessarily know what to say, you call me. You stay there.

And, just having you there makes things feel okay.

When I'm lying beside you, I feel like I don't want the moment to end. I wish I could lie beside you every day. I wish I could come home to you every day. I don't want to keep saying goodbye to you and going home without you. Being beside you feels so warm and safe, and with you, I feel like I don't have to be scared.

You have also taught me that I can be my truest self. I don't feel like I have to pretend to be someone else around you. I can be silly, weird, makulit, embarrassing, and completely myself. You even willingly take the blame for my farts. I don't know what kind of love that is, but I think that's pretty serious.

I'm also so incredibly proud of you.

You've been through so much, and you're still here. You're graduating as an ME, and I know how much strength it took for you to get here. I know this birthday is different because it's your first one without your mom, and I know there are things you carry that I may never fully understand. But I hope you know how amazed I am by you. I am proud of the person you are, proud of everything you've accomplished, and proud that even after everything you've been through, you continue to move forward.

And I hope you know that you don't always have to be strong by yourself. If there are days when you feel tired, you can rest with me. If you need someone to stay, I'll stay. If you need someone to listen, I'll listen. I want to be there for you the way you've been there for me.

Because you have become so much more than my boyfriend.

You're my baby, my mahal, my love, my Cubby. You're the person I want to tell everything to. You're the person I want beside me when something funny happens, when something hurts, when I'm excited, when I'm scared, and when nothing is happening at all.

I love imagining our future together.

I imagine us building our own house and probably arguing over the furniture because we have completely different tastes. I imagine us choosing our wall paint, decorating every room, traveling the world and going anywhere we want to go. I imagine the grocery trips, cooking together, watching movies in bed, having kids and pets, and eventually waking up early in the morning to watch the sunrise from our balcony while drinking hot chocolate because I know how much you love it.

I don't know exactly what our future will look like. But whenever I imagine it, you're there.

And that's what makes it feel warm.

I want to spend a very, very long time with you. I want to experience the boring days and the exciting ones. I want to grow with you, build a life with you, discover places with you, laugh with you, annoy you, be annoyed by you, and love you through all the versions of ourselves that we'll become.

If someday we're sitting on that balcony, much older, looking at the life we built together, I think I'll look at you and remember the boy who approached me with a sunflower, looking a little nervous, with the brightest smile.

And I'll want to tell you:

Thank you.

Thank you for finding me. Thank you for loving me. Thank you for making me feel safe enough to be myself. Thank you for staying. Thank you for all the little things you do that you might not even realize mean so much to me.

And most of all, thank you for saving me.

I don't know if you'll ever fully understand how much you mean to me, but I hope I spend the rest of our lives showing you.

Happy birthday, my baby.

I am so grateful that you're here. I'm so grateful that you're mine, and I'm so grateful that I get to love you.

I love you more than I know how to put into words.

And if heaven really did send you to me, then I hope I get to spend a very, very long time thanking heaven by loving you the best way I know how.

I love you, mahal. Always.
